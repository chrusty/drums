In 2022 I moved to a place where I suddenly had a lot of space. It occurred to me that I could use this space to do something impulsive, like buy a drum kit and try to play it. So I bought a basic electronic drum kit and started playing along to stuff like Black Sabbath and Cypress Hill, and soon realised that I quite enjoyed it.

Shortly after that my friend put me in touch with a local guy (Greg) who I did weekly lessons with for a year. He was big on rudiments, and every lesson started with 15 minutes of kung-fu doing sticking and rudiments together on practise pads, before moving to the drum kit. Unlike his younger students, I wasn't interested in learning songs, instead I was looking for the ingredients to come up with my own grooves. After a year he recommended that I start taking lessons with someone else who would be a great fit for the funk / jazz styles which I was interested in.
